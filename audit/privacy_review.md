# Publication-file content review

The November 5 tree contains public code, non-identifying aggregate results, genomic window inventories, binned LD summaries, synthetic examples, and separate historical/revised figures. Automated checks scan both TSV and CSV tables and reject sample/SNP identifier, genotype, per-variant beta/SE and PIP columns. Raw genotypes, phenotype values, animal IDs, full GRMs, raw LD pairs, detailed GWAS, credible-set membership and per-SNP fine-mapping calls are excluded.

Source provenance uses relative filenames and checksums. A scan of new sources, derived tables, documentation and audit records found no real private workstation or computing paths. The largest publication file is the approximately 7.3 MB pooled Manhattan PDF; all files are below 10 MB. Full private source inventories, internal plans and obsolete working reports are retained outside this tree. Scientific reference tables and historical estimators remain where required for reproducibility.

This is a content review, not a claim about institutional approval or governing data-access permissions. No new ethics, data-access or funding declaration has been invented. The revision record states the outstanding author-supplied access terms, incomplete GCTA fits, unavailable documents, citation-refresh and theoretical-input dependencies.
