Table 3. Analysis is restricted to BOLT-LMM and REGENIE-LOCO inputs under the diffuse polygenic architecture simulated here. All PIP ≥ 0.9 SNPs from the 13,301 converged locus fits in each pipeline were included; no lead-window stratification or top-SNP restriction was applied. Loci were selected using genome-wide significant lead SNPs (P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸). SNPs were counted once within each GWAS method, fine-mapping approach, and phenotype; counts across the 10 phenotypes are SNP–phenotype calls, not globally unique variants. Empirical precision is the number of simulated causal calls divided by all high-PIP calls. Classes use each SNP’s own causal status and position: causal; non-causal in effect windows; or null windows. The latter two classes together are non-causal. The mean assigned PIP is the average saved posterior probability among selected calls, shown as a model-implied causal fraction for comparison with the observed causal fraction; it is not an independent empirical estimate. MAF-matched expectations use 10 pool-specific MAF quantile bins and E_c = sum_k n_k p_k(c), with MAF from the 20,000 GWAS animals and pools restricted to SNPs analyzed for the same fine-mapping approach, GWAS method, and phenotype. Expected counts are summed across phenotypes before dividing by total calls. Counts and percentages are pooled across phenotypes. Causal O/E is the ratio of summed observed causal counts to summed MAF-matched expected causal counts. Rounding uses unrounded underlying values. No hypothesis tests or independence-based confidence intervals are reported.
