Residual-variance sensitivity of standard SuSiE-RSS. (a) Average precision among unique analyzed SNPs; dotted segments indicate the mean causal prevalence in each GWAS-specific evaluation pool. (b) Pooled precision of SNP calls with PIP ≥0.9 across ten phenotypes. (c) Percentage of nominal 95% credible sets containing at least one causal SNP. Points in a and c represent ten phenotypes, and horizontal segments represent means. (d) Median of the ten phenotype-level median residual variances, with the full locus-level minimum–maximum range. Fixed variance is one. Both runs use identical loci, GWAS statistics, allele alignment, ordinary in-sample LD, N=20,000, L=10, 95% credible sets, minimum absolute correlation 0.5, maximum 1,000 iterations, and seed 20261014. Only estimate_residual_variance changes. All 6,732 BOLT-LMM and 6,569 REGENIE-LOCO fits converged under both settings without warnings. Each SNP is counted once per phenotype and GWAS method, using its maximum PIP across overlapping loci. These are descriptive comparisons; correlated calls are not treated as independent replicates.
