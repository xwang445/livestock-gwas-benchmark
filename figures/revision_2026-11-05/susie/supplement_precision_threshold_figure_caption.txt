Causal precision across PIP thresholds. (a) Pooled precision over the full threshold range. The gray diagonal is the PIP threshold expressed as a percentage, a reference rather than observed precision. (b) Precision for thresholds from 0.90 to 0.9999. (c) Retained calls over the same high-PIP range. Markers show thresholds 0.90, 0.95 and 0.99. Colors identify BOLT-LMM and REGENIE-LOCO inputs; solid and dashed lines identify standard SuSiE-RSS and LOCO SuSiE-adj. Precision is causal calls divided by all calls with PIP at least the threshold. Counts pool ten phenotypes, with each SNP counted once per phenotype, GWAS method and pipeline, retaining its maximum PIP across overlapping loci. All 13,301 fits converged in both pipelines. Truth comprises the 10,000 simulated causal SNPs and enters only the evaluation. Both pipelines estimate residual variance. Standard RSS uses ordinary in-sample LD and N=20,000; SuSiE-adj uses LOCO-adjusted LD and locus-specific effective sample size. Other settings are L=10, 95% credible sets, minimum absolute correlation 0.5 and maximum 1,000 iterations. Thus the comparison changes LD and sample-size input together. Thresholds use 0.01 increments below 0.90, 0.001 increments from 0.90 to 0.999, plus 0.9995 and 0.9999. Exact PIP=1 is excluded because numerical saturation affects that endpoint. Curves are descriptive and do not assume independent SNP calls.
