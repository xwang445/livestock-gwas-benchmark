Causal precision of high-PIP fine-mapping calls. Bars show causal and noncausal proportions among SNP calls with PIP ≥0.9. White dashed lines mark the MAF-matched expected causal proportions; callouts give these values. Numbers inside bars are percentages, and numbers above bars are pooled observed-to-expected causal-count ratios. A SNP contributes once per phenotype, GWAS method and pipeline, using its maximum PIP across overlapping loci. Counts are summed over ten phenotypes before conversion to percentages. For each phenotype and pipeline, unique analyzed SNPs are partitioned into ten MAF quantile bins using frequencies from the 20,000 GWAS animals. The expected causal count is the sum, over bins, of the number of high-PIP calls multiplied by the causal proportion of the analyzed pool in that bin. This is an exact analytical expectation without Monte Carlo sampling. All calls in converged loci are included. Both pipelines use estimated residual variance. Standard SuSiE-RSS uses ordinary in-sample LD and N=20,000; SuSiE-adj uses LOCO-adjusted LD and locus-specific effective sample size. Both use L=10, 95% credible sets, minimum absolute correlation 0.5 and maximum 1,000 iterations. All 6,732 BOLT-LMM and 6,569 REGENIE-LOCO fits converged in each pipeline. The comparison changes LD and sample-size input together. No independence-based uncertainty intervals or significance tests are shown.
