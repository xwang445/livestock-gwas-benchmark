Figure 3. Significant loci and SNPs
Cells show detected 500 kb loci and significant SNPs in parentheses; P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸. Zero-anchored half-open windows retain chromosome-end fragments. All ten configurations have complete data for ten phenotypes.

Figure 5. Mean overlap of significant loci
The compact matrix shows the equal-phenotype mean Jaccard index, conditional on both locus sets being nonempty. Each cell states the number of defined phenotypes (n), excluding phenotypes with an empty locus set for either configuration. Pending cells also show n, not the number of completed fits. Averages are withheld until all ten phenotype outputs are available for both configurations. All ten phenotype-specific matrices are retained as Supplementary_phenotype_overlap_review.
Jaccard indices use the inclusive-threshold locus sets of Figure 3. As in the historical plotting convention, comparisons are undefined (NA) if either set is empty; in the phenotype-specific supplement, pending fits are marked by a dash. A set-theoretic alternative with zero for one empty set is supplied separately in the canonical table.

Figure 6. Regional spillover and genomic inflation
(a) Detected central-null 500 kb windows divided by 1,480 eligible windows, mean ± sample SD across ten phenotypes. (b) Historical central-null genomic inflation: median finite one-df association chi-square divided by 0.4549364, then mean ± SD across phenotypes; the P-inverse-survival estimate is supplied separately. (c,d) Significant/(tested × 5 × 10⁻⁸) is computed within nonempty 50 kb null windows, averaged across windows at mirrored distance indices, then equally across phenotypes. Panel d excludes BOLT-LMM and REGENIE LOCO. All ten configurations are aggregated over ten phenotypes. No tested marker lies at a 500 kb or central-null boundary. Spatial 50 kb bins preserve the historical closed endpoints, duplicating three shared-boundary markers per phenotype.

Table S1
A locus is a non-overlapping, chromosome-specific 500 kb genomic window containing at least one SNP with P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸. Windows are anchored at zero, [start,end), with terminal fragments retained. Counts are mean ± sample SD across all ten phenotypes. Percentages are the equally weighted mean ± sample SD of phenotype-specific percentages: detected loci of the stated region divided by all detected loci. Phenotypes with zero detected loci have undefined percentages and are excluded from percentage summaries; their number is reported. Central-null is a subset of null, not a third disjoint category. These percentages are not the central-null window detection rate in Figure 6a, which has a fixed denominator of 1,480 windows per phenotype. Pooled percentages are separate columns in the source TSV. GCTA aggregates include all ten phenotypes.
