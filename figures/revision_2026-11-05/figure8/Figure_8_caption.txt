Figure 8. Pooled precision–recall performance of fine-mapping LOCO associations. (a) BOLT-LMM and (b) REGENIE-LOCO inputs analyzed using SuSiE-adj and standard SuSiE-RSS. Each curve ranks SNP–phenotype pairs pooled across ten simulated phenotypes by posterior inclusion probability (PIP). Each SNP contributes once per phenotype, GWAS method and pipeline, retaining its maximum PIP across overlapping loci. The dotted line is the causal fraction of that analyzed pool and represents the precision baseline for random ranking. Average precision (AP) is the stepwise area under the full pooled curve; it is computed before selecting display vertices and differs from the mean of ten phenotype-specific AP values. Both pipelines estimate residual variance. Standard RSS uses ordinary in-sample LD and N=20,000; SuSiE-adj retains LOCO-adjusted LD and locus-specific effective sample sizes. Both use L=10, 95% credible sets, minimum absolute correlation 0.5 and maximum 1,000 iterations. All 6,732 BOLT-LMM and 6,569 REGENIE-LOCO locus fits converged in both pipelines. Loci were selected at P ≤5×10⁻⁸. Display vertices are sampled from the exact pooled curves, retaining endpoints and precision extrema; the zero-call endpoint is omitted because empirical precision is undefined. The comparison changes LD and sample-size inputs together. These descriptive curves do not treat correlated SNP calls as independent.
