Figure 2. Genomic windows for effect and null-region analyses

(a) The simulation alternates 2 Mb polygenic effect windows and 4 Mb null windows. Causal positions occur only in effect windows. Each null window contains a central 2 Mb region separated from either adjacent effect window by 1 Mb. Genome-wide detected loci are non-overlapping 500 kb windows; each complete central-null region contains four such windows. The illustrated 8 Mb segment includes two adjacent effect windows. (b) For fine-scale spatial analyses, each 4 Mb null window is partitioned into 80 consecutive 50 kb bins. Bins are indexed symmetrically from 1 to 40 by their distance from the nearest effect-window boundary. Ellipses omit intervening bins; the expanded bin diagram is schematic. Central-null regions are nested within null regions, rather than forming an additional disjoint region class.
