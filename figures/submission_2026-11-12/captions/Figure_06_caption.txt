Figure 6. Central-null spillover and spatial signal decay

(a) Fraction of 1,480 central-null 500-kb windows containing at least one SNP with P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸, shown as mean ± sample SD across ten phenotypes; labels give mean percentages. (b) Central-null genomic inflation, shown as mean ± sample SD across phenotypes. The historical Figure 6b estimator is retained: median finite one-degree-of-freedom association χ² divided by 0.4549364, using (β/SE)² when coefficients and standard errors are available. The dashed line marks λGC = 1. (c) Observed-to-expected significant-SNP counts in 50-kb null windows, with expected count equal to the tested-SNP count multiplied by 5 × 10⁻⁸. Ratios are averaged equally over nonempty windows at each mirrored distance index, then equally across ten phenotypes. Distances are bin midpoints measured from the nearer simulated effect boundary. The historical closed-endpoint convention is retained; source tables document boundary cases. (d) The same calculation excluding BOLT-LMM and REGENIE_LOCO, using its own vertical scale. Dotted reference lines in c and d indicate observed/expected = 1. Symbols and line styles supplement the shared method colors.
