Figure 1. Linkage disequilibrium decay across the pig genome

Mean pairwise r² was calculated in identical 10 kb distance bins from the 135,358-SNP panel in all 31,301 animals. Each retained SNP pair receives equal weight within its bin. Gray points show bin means; the black line is the shared fitted curve. The spline was fitted once to the complete 0–10 Mb set of bin means, weighting bins equally, and reused for both views: (a) 0–2 Mb and (b) 0–10 Mb. Bins are left-closed and right-open, with 10 Mb retained in the last bin. The first bin mean is 0.5925 at the 5 kb midpoint, rather than an estimate at zero distance.

Figure 2. Genomic windows for effect and null-region analyses

(a) The simulation alternates 2 Mb polygenic effect windows and 4 Mb null windows. Causal positions occur only in effect windows. Each null window contains a central 2 Mb region separated from either adjacent effect window by 1 Mb. Genome-wide detected loci are non-overlapping 500 kb windows; each complete central-null region contains four such windows. The illustrated 8 Mb segment includes two adjacent effect windows. (b) For fine-scale spatial analyses, each 4 Mb null window is partitioned into 80 consecutive 50 kb bins. Bins are indexed symmetrically from 1 to 40 by their distance from the nearest effect-window boundary. Ellipses omit intervening bins; the expanded bin diagram is schematic. Central-null regions are nested within null regions, rather than forming an additional disjoint region class.

Figure 3. Significant loci and SNPs across simulated phenotypes

Each cell shows the number of detected loci above the number of significant SNPs in parentheses. Loci are chromosome-specific, zero-anchored 500-kb windows containing at least one SNP with P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸; terminal fragments are retained. Color represents detected-locus count, using a logarithmic scale for count plus one. All ten configurations use the same 20,000 animals, 135,358 markers and ten phenotype replicates. SLEMM uses the updated benchmark configuration. REGENIE_LOCO and REGENIE_woLOCO denote analyses with and without leave-one-chromosome-out prediction, respectively. NYC and Kansas identify the corresponding FarmCPU and BLINK implementations. The source table contains all 100 count pairs.

Figure 4. Significant association signals across ten simulated phenotypes

Rows show phenotypes 1–10. Columns show BOLT-LMM and REGENIE_LOCO (left), FarmCPU and BLINK with their NYC and Kansas configurations (middle), and fastGWA, REGENIE_woLOCO, SLEMM and GCTA-MLMA (right). All panels use the same −log₁₀(P) range of 0–110 and retain every observed peak. Points show SNPs with P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸; dashed lines mark this threshold. Colors and shapes identify configurations. Chromosomes 1–18 are concatenated. Full-genome plots of all tested SNPs are provided in Figures S1–S10.

Figure 5. Phenotype-specific overlap of detected loci

Each matrix shows the indicated phenotype. Full method names on the left and beneath the bottom matrices define the common row and column ordering. Cell color gives the Jaccard index (intersection/union) for detected 500-kb loci at P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸. Grey denotes NA when either locus set is empty. SLEMM and GCTA-MLMA have no detected loci in phenotypes 2, 6, 8, 9 and 10. Numerically annotated matrices are supplied in Figures S11–S20.

Figure 6. Central-null spillover and spatial signal decay

(a) Fraction of 1,480 central-null 500-kb windows containing at least one SNP with P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸, shown as mean ± sample SD across ten phenotypes; labels give mean percentages. (b) Central-null genomic inflation, shown as mean ± sample SD across phenotypes. The historical Figure 6b estimator is retained: median finite one-degree-of-freedom association χ² divided by 0.4549364, using (β/SE)² when coefficients and standard errors are available. The dashed line marks λGC = 1. (c) Observed-to-expected significant-SNP counts in 50-kb null windows, with expected count equal to the tested-SNP count multiplied by 5 × 10⁻⁸. Ratios are averaged equally over nonempty windows at each mirrored distance index, then equally across ten phenotypes. Distances are bin midpoints measured from the nearer simulated effect boundary. The historical closed-endpoint convention is retained; source tables document boundary cases. (d) The same calculation excluding BOLT-LMM and REGENIE_LOCO, using its own vertical scale. Dotted reference lines in c and d indicate observed/expected = 1. Symbols and line styles supplement the shared method colors.

Figure 7. Predicted and observed association statistics for LOCO methods

The 135,358 SNPs were grouped into 20 equal-count bins using the cached theoretical non-centrality parameter (NCP) from Equation 13. Each point compares the mean predicted statistic, 1 + NCP, with the observed mean association χ² for (a) BOLT-LMM and (b) REGENIE_LOCO. Observed values average the ten phenotype-specific bin means. Vertical error bars are 95% confidence intervals calculated as the mean ± t₉,₀.₉₇₅ × sample SD/√10. The dashed line is the identity line. Each panel has equal physical x- and y-axis lengths and common 0–70 limits. Predictions and bin assignments are retained from the original cached analysis; the figure is redrawn from the saved aggregate values.

Figure 8. Fine-mapping ranking and recovery by recovered causal PVE

(a) Precision–recall curves for BOLT-LMM and REGENIE_LOCO loci, ranking SNP–phenotype pairs pooled across ten phenotypes by PIP. SuSiE-adj and SuSiE-RSS both use estimate_residual_variance = TRUE. Each SNP contributes once per phenotype and pipeline, retaining its maximum PIP across loci. AP denotes pooled stepwise average precision; dotted gray lines mark random-ranking precision, equal to causal prevalence in each analyzed pool. Precision is displayed from 0% to 16%; all empirical curve values fall within this range. The artificial zero-call endpoint is omitted. (b) Fraction of causal SNPs reaching PIP ≥0.9, grouped by author-supplied recovered PVE relative to the design expectation h²/m = 0.5/10,000 = 0.005%. Points are equal-phenotype mean call rates, and error bars are 95% t-based confidence intervals across ten phenotypes. Colored dotted lines give the corresponding non-causal call rates for each pipeline. Parentheses show analyzed causal SNP–phenotype counts. Recovered PVE is distinct from PVE estimated using GWAS association coefficients. Standard RSS uses ordinary in-sample LD and N = 20,000; SuSiE-adj uses LOCO-adjusted LD and locus-specific effective sample sizes. Both use L = 10, 95% credible sets, minimum absolute correlation 0.5 and at most 1,000 iterations. All 6,732 BOLT-LMM and 6,569 REGENIE_LOCO fits converged in each pipeline. Exact pooled metrics and phenotype-specific PVE-group summaries accompany the figure.
