## Results

All ten configurations have results for all ten phenotypes. With P ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁸, SLEMM detects 71 significant SNPs in 16 phenotype-specific loci and GCTA MLMA detects 58 SNPs in 15 loci. Their phenotype-mean Jaccard is 0.750 over five nonempty pairs; pooled intersection/union is 13/18. Their completed association tables are unchanged by this figure update.

The [recovered-effect analysis](recovered_effects_2026-11-11.md) adds the supplied PVE definition without treating recovered effects as original simulation draws. SLEMM has 11 causal leads among 16 detected loci (68.75%); GCTA MLMA has 10/15 (66.67%). Median recovered PVE among causal leads is 2.36464 and 2.38336 times the expected per-causal-SNP PVE, respectively. The pooled causal-lead fractions for BOLT-LMM and REGENIE_LOCO are 1,519/16,002 (9.49256%) and 1,432/15,450 (9.26861%). Lead association coefficients and recovered causal effects define different PVE estimators and are tabulated separately.

The final Figure 8 contains pooled precision–recall curves for BOLT-LMM and REGENIE-LOCO plus detection by recovered-PVE group. Both final pipelines use residual-variance estimation TRUE. Final RSS high-PIP counts are 2,021 and 2,072, with 261 and 254 causal calls. The supervisor's earlier RSS counts of 2,009 and 2,064 belong to the preserved FALSE baseline. Exact average precision comes from the complete rankings; reduced display vertices serve only to redraw the curves.

## Scope and remaining provenance

The author-supplied recovered-effect table is now available locally and supports the published aggregate PVE analyses. Its generating code and original simulation effect draws remain unverified. No recovery procedure is inferred from the supplied table. The actual Equation 12 generator, variant-level theoretical cache and covariance normalization underlying Figure 7 remain unavailable; redrawing retained bin summaries does not validate that upstream calculation. Historical GAPIT version attribution remains unresolved.

The shared distance-qualified high-PIP null-summary script is included as a portable calculator with an explicit framework input. Its ≥200/500/1,000 kb statistics depend on the original framework's distance-to-boundary values. That file is unavailable locally, so no inferred distance-summary table is presented as an executed result.

The update distributes no animal IDs, genotypes, phenotypes, raw GWAS, per-SNP effects/PIPs, detailed lead calls, raw LD/GRMs, supervisor correspondence or manuscript files. See [data availability](data_availability.md) and [publication review](../audit/privacy_review.md).
